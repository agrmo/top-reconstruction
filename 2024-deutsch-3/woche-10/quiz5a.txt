Lektion 26 – Schreiben

You are a witness to a murder crime and you are currently being held in a police station for interrogation (like in an episode of Tatort!). The detective leading the investigation asks you to provide a comprehensive account of what you have witnessed. However, since you were found next to the body, you are strongly being considered as a possible suspect. Whether you are guilty or not is up to you! In any case, you tell the investigator your side of the story, and your aim is to make your account of the crime scene as convincing as possible to remove yourself as a suspect.

Tell your story in as much detail as you can, and make it believable – you want to clear your name from suspicion, and too many gaps in the story will land you in jail (!!!). Make sure to properly set the scene, describe who was around at the time of the crime, and structure the story well (Use Präteritum or Perfekt!). Be sure to include:

    Proper greeting
    The situation:
        Who was murdered?
        What connection do you have to the murder victim?
        What motive could there have been for the murder?
        Where and when did you find the body?
        What were you doing when you found the body?
        Were any suspicious items or clues about the murder near the body?
        Was there a weapon?
    Then, speculate on the possible identity of the perpetrator. Make sure the detective doesn’t leave suspecting you!
    End with an appropriate salutation.
