\section*{Problem 8.b.}
The function in question is
\e{f(x)=\begin{cases}0 \quad &-1\leq x < 0\\ 1 \quad &0\leq x \leq 1\end{cases}}
Write down the Legendre expansion of $f(x)$ with coefficients $B_j$, and simplify using Problem 6, 7.
\e{B_j=\fr{2j+1}{2}\int_{-1}^1 f(x)P_j(x) dx=\fr{2j+1}{2}\int_0^1 P_j(x) dx \quad j=1,2,...}
From 6.b. we see that $B_j=0$ for $j=2,4,...$, so simplify $B_j$, then apply Problem 8.a.
\e{B_j \quad j=1,2,...\rightarrow B_{2n+1} \quad n=0,1,...}
\e{B_{2n+1}&=\fr{2(2n+1)+1}{2}\int_0^1 P_{2n+1}(x)dx\quad n=0,1,2,...\\
&=\fr{4n+3}{2}\int_0^1 P_{2n+1}(x)dx\\
&= \boxed{\fr{(4n+3)(-1)^n (2n)!}{2^{2n+2} (n!)^2 (n+1)}\quad n=0,1,2,...}}
Plug into the Legendre expansion of $f(x)$.
\e{f(x)&=\sum_{j=0}^\infty B_j P_j(x)=B_0 P_0 + \sum_{j=1}^\infty B_j P_j(x)
\intertext{From the notes $B_0=\fr{1}{2}$. Simplify the series from $j=1$ to $\infty$ to $n=0$ to $\infty$, like before.}
&= \fr{1}{2} + \sum_{n=0}^\infty B_{2n+1}P_{2n+1}(x)\\
&= \fr{1}{2}+\sum_{n=0}^\infty \fr{(4n+3)(-1)^n (2n)!}{2^{2n+2} (n!)^2 (n+1)}
\intertext{Distribute the $2^2$.}
&= \boxed{\fr{1}{2}+\sum_{n=0}^\infty \fr{4n+3}{4n+4} \fr{(-1)^n (2n)!}{2^{2n} (n!)^2}}}
By writing $f(x)$ as a Legendre series and simplifying using Problems 6, 7, 8.a., we get the desired result.
