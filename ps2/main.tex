\section*{Question 2.a.}
We will make use of these.
\e{\label{adnf}A_\mu\rightarrow U A_\mu \ui +i\gi U \p_\mu \ui}
\e{\label{asjd}q\rightarrow Uq\quad \bq\rightarrow \bq\ui}
\e{\label{ddx}\dds=\ds+if\as}
\e{\label{uu}U^\dagger U = UU^\dagger=1}
\e{\p_\mu (\ui U)=\p_\mu (1)=0}
\e{\p_\mu (\ui U)=(\p_\mu \ui)U+\ui \p_\mu U=0}
\e{\label{akjsdn}(\p_\mu \ui)U=-\ui \p_\mu U}
Write $\bq\cancel{D}q$ and use \eref{adnf} and \eref{asjd}.
\e{\bq\dds q&=\bq(\ds+if\as)q\\
&\rightarrow \bq \ui (\ds+if(U\as \ui + i\gi U \ds \ui))Uq
\intertext{Expand. Note $\ds q \rightarrow \ds(Uq)=\ds U + \ds q$. The $i^2$ gives one minus sign.}
&=\bq\ui \ds U q + \bq \ui U \ds q + if\bq \ui U \as \ui U q - f\gi \bq \ui U \ds \ui U q
\intertext{Use \eref{uu}.}
&=\bq\ui \ds U q + \bq \ds q + if\bq \as q - f\gi \bq \ds \ui U q
\intertext{Use \eref{akjsdn}. All terms are positive again.}
&=\bq\ui \ds U q + \bq \ds q + if\bq \as q + f\gi \bq \ui \ds U q
\intertext{Factor.}
&=\bq (\ds+if\as) q + (1+f\gi)\bq \ui \ds U q
\intertext{For the term to be invariant, it must be that $f=-g$.}
&=\bq \dds q}
\e{\boxed{f=-g}}
By simultaneously transforming $q$ and $A$ in $\bq \cancel{D} q$ and simplifying we find $f=-g$ for $\bq \dds q$ to be invariant under both transformations. Because the calculation is straightforward I would like to disagree with $f=g$. For example, refer to Peskin and Schroeder Eq.~15.45 and Eq.~15.46 to show they are negatives for any continuous non-Abelian gauge group.
\section*{Question 2.b.}
We will make use of these.
\e{\label{9128n3}A_{[\mu}B_{\nu]}=A_\mu B_\nu-A_\nu B_\mu=-A_{[\nu}B_{\mu]}}
\e{\label{192n3}A_{[\mu}B_{\nu]}+A_{[\mu}B_{\nu]}=A_{[\mu}B_{\nu}+A_\mu B_{\nu]}}
\e{\label{cc}U\p_{[\mu} \p_\nu \ui + U\p_\nu \p_{\mu]}\ui=0}
Write $F$ in its antisymmetrized form, ignoring the $\fr{1}{n!}$ because it is irrelevant to the result.
\e{F_{\mu\nu}&=\p_\mu A_\nu - \p_\nu A_\mu + ih(A_\mu A_\nu - A_\nu A_\mu)\\&=\p_{[\mu}A_{\nu]}+ihA_{[\mu}A_{\nu]}\\&=\p_{[\mu}A_{\nu}+ihA_{\mu}A_{\nu]}
\intertext{Use \eref{adnf}.}
&\rightarrow \p_{[\mu}(U A_\nu \ui +i\gi U \p_{\nu} \ui)+ih(U A_{\mu} \ui + i\gi U \p_\mu \ui)(U A_\nu \ui + i \gi U U \p_{\nu]} \ui)
\intertext{Distribute.}
&= \p_{[\mu}(U A_\nu \ui)+i\gi\p_\mu (U\p_\nu \ui)+ih(UA_{\mu}\ui U A_\nu \ui + UA_\mu \ui (i\gi U \p_\nu \ui) \\&\quad+ i\gi U \p_\mu \ui U A_\nu \ui + (i\gi)^2U\p_\mu \ui U \p_{\nu]}\ui)
\intertext{Distribute.}
&=\p_{[\mu} U A_\nu \ui U\p_\mu A_\nu \ui + UA_\nu \p_\mu \ui + i\gi (\p_\mu U \p_\nu \ui + U\p_\mu \p_\nu \ui + U\p_\nu \p_{\mu}\ui)\\
&\quad +ih(UA_{\mu} \ui U A_\nu \ui + i\gi U A_\mu \ui U \p_\nu \ui+i\gi U \p_\mu \ui U A_\nu \ui \\&\quad +(i\gi)^2U\p_\mu \ui U \p_{\nu]}\ui))\\
\intertext{Use \eref{uu} and \eref{cc}. Definitely do not use $(\p_\mu U) \ui= \p_\mu (1)$.}
&=\p_{[\mu} U A_\nu \ui + \boxed{U\p_\mu A_\nu \ui} + U A_\nu \p_\mu \ui + i\gi \p_\mu U\p_{\nu} \ui\\
&\quad +ih(\boxed{UA_{\mu} A_\nu \ui} + i\gi U A_\mu \p_\nu \ui + i\gi U \p_\mu \ui U A_\nu \ui + (i\gi)^2 U \p_\mu \ui U \p_{\nu]} \ui)\\
\intertext{Factor out $F$ by collecting the two boxed terms. Distribute $ih$, giving minus signs.}
&=UF_{\mu\nu} \ui + \p_{[\mu} U A_\nu \ui + U A_\nu \p_\mu \ui + i\gi \p_\mu U \p_\nu \ui\\
&\quad -h\gi U A_\mu \p_\nu \ui - h\gi U \p_\mu \ui U A_\nu \ui - h\gi (i\gi) U \p_\mu \ui U \p_{\nu]} \ui
\intertext{Use \eref{akjsdn} and \eref{9128n3}. Every term is positive again, and $\mu$ and $\nu$ indices are aligned.}
&= UF_{\mu\nu} \ui + \p_{[\mu}U A_\nu \ui + U A_\nu \p_\mu \ui + i\gi \p_\mu U \p_\nu \ui\\
&\quad +h\gi U A_\nu \p_\mu \ui + h\gi \p_\mu U A_\nu \ui + h\gi (i\gi) \p_\mu U \p_{\nu]} \ui
\intertext{Factor out $(1+h\gi)$, which would not be possible if the indices were misaligned.}
&=\label{12n} UF_{\mu\nu} \ui + (1+h\gi)(\p_{[\mu} U A_\nu \ui + UA_\nu \p_\mu \ui + (i\gi)\p_\mu U \p_{\nu]}\ui)}
\e{\boxed{h=-g}}
By applying the non-Abelian gauge transformation on the non-Abelian $F_{\mu\nu}$, and carefully simplifying, we find $F$ is invariant when $h=-g$. I am fairly confident in this result, so I would like to disagree with $h=g$. For example, refer to Cheng and Li 8.45 and 8.51 to see they are negatives for any continuous non-Abelian gauge group.
